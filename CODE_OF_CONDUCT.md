# Code of Conduct

VeriCorpus welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful, focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people, avoid harassment or discriminatory language, and assume good faith while remaining precise about technical claims. Maintainers may remove content or participation that disrupts a safe and productive project environment.

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately through the repository's available moderation or security channels.
